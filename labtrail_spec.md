## Client Feature Request

Patients need one private, source-linked history of laboratory results collected across providers. LabTrail should let patients upload PDF or image reports, review and correct extracted result candidates before accepting them, and view accepted results over time with their source and comparability caveats. Patients should be able to share a selected, read-only subset with a named clinician for a limited time, and revoke access. The system must not interpret results; the patient controls what enters the record and who can see it.

## Candidate Classes

- Patient
- LabReport
- LabResult
- ShareGrant
